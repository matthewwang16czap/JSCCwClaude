WHY THIS WAVE (docs/ASSESSMENT.md). Three questions decide what the paper can claim:

  G-A  matched compute. The wave-1 ViT that beats Swin uses 27x its parameters and
       16x its FLOPs. Does a ViT with token transmission still win at the SAME
       compute? ViT p8 384x6 (21.6 M, 43.9 GFLOPs) against Swin large (12.7 M,
       41.8 GFLOPs, the published SwinJSCC size class), both from scratch, wave-1
       protocol.
  G-B  per-image allocation (the "adaptive" half of AdaTok's flow). At a fixed mean
       rate, how much does giving each image its own token budget gain? The
       cross-fitted oracle on Kodak, for the wave-1 ViT and Swin (prefix models).
  G-C  budget-specialised decoding. How big is the multi-rate penalty under the c1
       protocol (1/8 and 1/48 specialists), how much of it a specialised DECODER
       alone can recover (1/8 with the encoder frozen: the ceiling for any
       decoder-side head), and how much AdaTok's per-budget LoRA heads recover, alone
       and stacked with the linear exits; plus a second seed of the c1 control and of
       the c1 linear exits.

  id   run                                            protocol              answers
  M1   vit p8 384x6 s4, from scratch                   2000 epochs, uniform  G-A
  M2   swin large, from scratch                        2000 epochs, uniform  G-A
  A1   allocation sweep + oracle, wave-1 vit           Kodak, no training    G-B
  A2   allocation sweep + oracle, wave-1 swin          Kodak, no training    G-B
  V1   vit 1/8 specialist                              c1 fine-tune          G-C: the gap at 1/8
  V2   vit 1/8, encoder frozen at w1 (decoder trains)  c1 fine-tune          G-C: the decoder's share
  V4   vit 1/48 specialist                             c1 fine-tune          G-C: the gap at 1/48
  W1   swin 1/8 specialist                             c1 fine-tune          G-C (swin)
  W2   swin 1/48 specialist                            c1 fine-tune          G-C (swin)
  L1   vit + LoRA budget heads (FiLM + LoRA, rank 16)  c1 fine-tune, grid    G-C: AdaTok's heads
  L2   vit + LoRA budget heads + linear exits          c1 fine-tune, grid    G-C: the stack
  R1   vit c1 control, seed 43                         c1 fine-tune, grid    seed noise
  R2   vit linear exits, seed 43                       c1 fine-tune, grid    does c1 replicate?

  card   queue (in order)                                           about
  GPU 2  M1                                                         the probe says (section 2)
  GPU 3  M2                                                         the probe says (section 2)

  (a c1 fine-tune is ~3.8 h for vit and ~2.7 h for swin by your logs; a run with a
